Played Nxd5 Best Rf1

Playing Nxd5 won a pawn but left the f2 square completely undefended. Black's rook on f8 could immediately capture on f2, forking your king and winning material back with interest. The engine suggests Rf1 first — defending the weakness before going pawn-hunting. In chess, king safety takes priority over material gain.
